About

I treat my degree as the first stone of something larger: a body of work, a reputation, and in time a name worth inheriting.

I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Uganda Christian University. Web and mobile development drew me in because they reward the same habits I want in every part of life: patience, precision and care for the person on the other side of the screen.

I don't want to simply finish projects. I want to finish them well, understand why they work, and leave them in a state another developer could pick up with ease. A good builder is also a good student, so I keep learning in the open and let each project raise the standard for the next.

How I build

Made to last

Plain, well-organised code that stays readable a year from now. I prefer a simple structure done properly over a clever one that breaks.

Refined, not decorated

Every element must earn its place. Good spacing, steady type and a restrained palette do more than effects ever will.

Built for people

Pages should load quickly, read well on a small phone and make sense at first glance, because real visitors are rarely patient or on fast connections.
